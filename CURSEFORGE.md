**Summary:** Sends your crafting materials and Bind on Equip items to your alts. You pick the categories, press Start, then Send.
**Categories:** Mail, Professions

## Description

Sending your crafting materials to the right alt, one item at a time, gets old fast. Alt Courier fills the mail for you: pick who gets what, press Start, check the mail and press Send. It never sends anything by itself, and attaches nothing until you press Start.

[![Support me on Ko-fi](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=storage.ko-fi.com/cdn/kofi5.png&h=36)](https://ko-fi.com/artisangw)

## What it does
- Add and remove categories: Bind on Equip, each profession's materials (Herbalism, Mining, Skinning, Tailoring, Cooking, Enchanting, Jewelcrafting, Engineering, Inscription), other crafting reagents, and everything else
- Each category goes to the alt you pick, or stays in your bags with no alt
- Only one bank alt? Give every category the same alt: it all goes out in as few mails as possible
- Press Start to fill the mail; after each send the next batch is filled, until nothing is left or you press Stop
- It steps back if the mail already holds something, or if you type in it
- Mail characters on your other accounts: turn it on in Settings, agree to the warning, then add each character by name. Mail can't be taken back, so check the names
- Never send an item, or anything with a word in its name
- Add an alt you haven't logged into yet
- Looks like the game's own windows

## Commands
- /ac list: who gets what, and which alts are known
- /ac add First Last: add an alt
- /ac settings: open the settings (mailing other accounts)
- /ac remove Name: forget an alt
- /ac exclude word or [item]: never send it
- /ac include word or [item]: send it again

It's a simple tool with no dependencies: it uses only the game.
