# Alt Courier

## 0.1.0

First release, an early version: bugs are expected. Reports are welcome.

- A panel beside the mailbox, built from the game's own window pieces
- Nothing is attached until you press Start: open the Send Mail tab, press Start, check the mail and press Send; the next batch follows until nothing is left, or you press Stop
- If the mail already holds something, or you type in it, Alt Courier steps back
- Categories you add and remove: Bind on Equip, one for each profession's materials (Herbalism, Mining, Skinning, Tailoring, Cooking, Enchanting, Jewelcrafting, Engineering, Inscription), other crafting reagents, and everything else
- One bank alt is enough: give every category the same alt and they go out together
- A category with no alt keeps its items in your bags
- Mail characters on your other accounts: tick the box in Settings and agree in the popup, then add each character by name
- Never send an item, or anything with a word in its name: /ac exclude
- Add an alt you haven't logged into yet: /ac add First Last
- Uses only the game: no other addon needed
